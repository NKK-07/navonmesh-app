03  /  DESIGN DECISIONS

Four decisions that make it work.

D-01

100% solar, off-grid

2.4 kW array into an MPPT controller and a 48 V LiFePO₄ pack. No grid, no diesel, no bill.

D-02

200 kg PUF chamber

100 mm foam and a sealed door hold 90–95% RH through the monsoon.

D-03

Eight languages, read aloud

Every screen and alert, spoken for farmers who do not read screens.

D-04

LoRa first, offline always

868 MHz telemetry where towers do not reach, SMS when it fails.
