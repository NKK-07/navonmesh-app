SECTION 03  /  BUILT FOR NER

A cold room designed for rain, cloud and bad roads.

Standard solar cold storage is designed for dry, sunny, flat, grid-adjacent India. The North East is none of those things. Four constraints shaped every part of this build.

SOLAR IRRADIANCE 3–5 kWh per m² per day
AMBIENT HUMIDITY 70–90 % relative humidity
TRANSPORTABLE MASS 200 kg fits a pickup truck
SITE SERVICES None no grid, no internet
