SECTION 03  /  BUILT FOR NER

A cold room designed for rain, cloud and bad roads.

Standard solar cold storage is designed for dry, sunny, flat, grid-adjacent India. The North East is none of those things. Four constraints shaped every part of this build.

SOLAR IRRADIANCE3–5 kWhper m² per day
AMBIENT HUMIDITY70–90 %relative humidity
TRANSPORTABLE MASS200 kgfits a pickup truck
SITE SERVICESNoneno grid, no internet
THE FOUR CONSTRAINTS
PROBLEM CONSTRAINT 01

Not enough sun

Heavy monsoon rainfall and dense cloud over hilly terrain cut peak solar hours well below what a plains installation counts on.

RESPONSE

The unit pairs a LiFePO4 battery with a 70 kg phase-change thermal store, so cooling continues through cloud and darkness.

SECTION 03.2  /  THE FIFTH CONSTRAINT

A cold room nobody can read the screen of is a cold room nobody uses.
