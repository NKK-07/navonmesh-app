03  /  DESIGN DECISIONS

Four decisions that make it work where nothing else does.

D-01

100% solar, off-grid

2.4 kW array into an MPPT controller and a 48 V LiFePO₄ pack. No grid connection, no diesel, no monthly bill.

D-02

200 kg PUF chamber

100 mm polyurethane foam and a hermetic seal hold 90–95% RH without letting the monsoon in, or condensation onto the produce.

D-03

Eight languages, read aloud

Hindi, Assamese, Nepali, Meitei, Khasi, Mizo, Nagamese and English, with speech output for farmers who do not read screens.

D-04

LoRa first, offline always

868 MHz LoRa carries telemetry where cell towers do not reach, with SMS fallback and an app that keeps working with no signal at all.

05  /  OPERATORS

One cold room, three people who depend on it.

ROLE 01

The farmer

Opens the app in their own language, hears the status read aloud, and sees how many days each batch has left before it must be sold.

  • Batch-by-batch shelf life
  • Sell-first alerts before spoilage
  • Keeps working with the phone offline
ROLE 02

The retailer

Sees what is in the chamber, how cold it has been kept and how long it will hold, so produce is bought on evidence rather than a guess at the mandi gate.

  • Live stock and weight
  • Cold-chain history per batch
  • Freshness proof before loading
ROLE 03

The FPO

Runs a cluster of units across villages from one screen: uptime, utilisation, member batches and which chamber needs a technician this week.

  • Multi-unit fleet view
  • Member-wise storage records
  • Maintenance scheduling
