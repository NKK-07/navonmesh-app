SECTION 04  /  IMPACT PROJECTED, NOT MEASURED

The loss is not the harvest. It is the week the farmer cannot wait.

Produce that has to be sold the day it is picked is sold at whatever price the day offers. Storage buys time, and time is where the money is.

SECTION 04.2  /  WHERE THE VALUE COMES FROM

Same harvest. Two very different weeks.

A 200 kg load of cabbage picked on a Monday, in a shed and in a chamber. The produce does not change. What changes is how long the farmer gets to choose a buyer.

  • The distress sale disappears, because the crop is no longer the thing under time pressure.
  • Batches can be aggregated across members until a lot is worth a buyer's trip.
  • Cold-chain history travels with the batch, so quality can be priced instead of argued about.
SECTION 04.3  /  METHOD

How these numbers were arrived at.

M-01  /  SHELF LIFE

Cold and ambient shelf life come from the ten crop profiles built into the system, one per NER crop, with their own set-points and humidity bands.

M-02  /  VALUE

Income retention is modelled from the difference between a same-day distress sale and a sale inside the extended window. It is a projection, not an audited figure.

M-03  /  WHAT IS MISSING

No unit has completed a field season yet. Every number on this page is a simulation from the prototype control panel, and will be replaced by pilot data.
