WITHOUT COLD

Sell on the day, at the day's price.

Everyone harvests in the same fortnight, so everyone sells in the same fortnight. The market knows it. Produce that cannot wait has no bargaining position, and a quarter to a third of what the North East grows never gets sold at all.

THE WORD FOR IT IS A DISTRESS SALE

WITH A UNIT ON THE ROOF

Wait for the price worth taking.

Eight degrees held through the night on solar, with a phase change store that carries another 8.6 hours after the sun goes. The harvest stops being perishable for long enough that selling becomes a decision rather than a deadline.

03 / HOW IT HOLDS

Four things, and no grid connection.

  1. 01

    2.4 kW on the roof

    A rooftop array into an MPPT controller. No separate land, no grid connection, no diesel, no monthly bill.

  2. 02

    48 V LiFePO4

    The battery carries the evening and most of the night. Lithium iron phosphate because it takes heat and deep cycling better than the alternatives.

  3. 03

    100 mm PUF, sealed

    The envelope keeps 90 to 95 percent humidity in and the monsoon out. A 70 kg phase change store holds the cold for another 8.6 hours once the compressor stops.

  4. 04

    868 MHz LoRa, then SMS

    Readings travel where there is no cell signal. The app works with no signal at all, in eight languages, and reads itself aloud for anyone who does not read a screen.

04 / WHO OWNS ONE

Nobody buys this alone.

The objection is never whether cold storage works. It is the upfront cost for a smallholder, and who fixes it when it breaks. The answer the sector has landed on is shared ownership, which is why the app has an FPO fleet view built into it.

  • The FPO owns the unitOne unit serves a collection point rather than a single farm, so the cost per farmer per season is a fraction of one distress sale.
  • Members share the chamberEvery batch is tracked to its owner, with its own crop profile and its own countdown.
  • It reports its own healthNine sensors over LoRa. A technician is told what is wrong before they set out, which matters when the nearest one is two valleys away.
