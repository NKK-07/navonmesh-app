SCROLL TO EXPLORE
ASSEMBLED
01

Solar Array

2.4 kW · Rooftop-mounted

Six panels on the roof generate all the power the unit needs. Sized against NER irradiance of 3–5 kWh/m²/day, not a sunny plains average.

02

MPPT Controller

48V DC Bus · Continuous tracking

Tracks the array through broken monsoon cloud to extract every watt. Protects the battery bank from over-charge and deep discharge.

03

Battery Bank

48V / 100 Ah · LiFePO₄

Lithium iron phosphate handles deep cycling and heat without a ventilated battery room. BMS reports health to the app.

04

ESP32 Controller

Dual Core · LoRa 868 MHz

Reads every sensor, decides compressor speed, raises alerts, and pushes telemetry over LoRa. The only part that makes decisions.

05

BLDC Compressor

~420 W · 42% duty cycle

Variable-speed brushless DC runs off the DC bus. No inrush surge means a smaller array and battery can do the job.

06

Thermal Store

70 kg PCM · 8.6 hrs reserve

Phase-change material freezes on surplus solar and releases cold after dark. Storing cold is far cheaper than storing the electricity to make it.

07

Cold Chamber

200 kg · 100 mm PUF

Hermetic polyurethane envelope holds 90–95% humidity. Sized to fit on a pickup truck so it can reach high-altitude collection centres.

DESIGN DECISIONS

Four decisions that make it work where nothing else does.

D-01

100% solar, off-grid

2.4 kW array into an MPPT controller and a 48 V LiFePO₄ pack. No grid connection, no diesel, no monthly bill.

D-02

200 kg PUF chamber

100 mm polyurethane foam and a hermetic seal hold 90–95% RH without letting the monsoon in.

D-03

Eight languages, read aloud

Hindi, Assamese, Nepali, Meitei, Khasi, Mizo, Nagamese and English, with speech output.

D-04

LoRa first, offline always

868 MHz LoRa carries telemetry where cell towers do not reach. The app works fully offline.
